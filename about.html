About Mini Games Hub

A free collection of 19 browser mini games — no download, no login, no paywalls.

What is Mini Games Hub?

Mini Games Hub is a free, browser-based gaming platform built entirely with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. We host 19 hand-crafted mini-games you can play instantly in your browser — on desktop or mobile — without signing up or installing anything.

The site loads fast, and minimal ads help us keep everything free for everyone.

We wanted a fast, distraction-free gaming hub where anyone can enjoy classic games without popups, forced logins, or paywalls. Mini Games Hub is a passion project focused on clean design, genuine gameplay, and accessibility for all.
