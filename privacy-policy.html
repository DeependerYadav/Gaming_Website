1. Information We Collect

Mini Games Hub is a client-side only website. We do not run a back-end server that stores user data. We do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Account credentials or passwords
  • Payment information
  • Location data
  • Personal identifiers of any kind

The only data that persists is your mini game high scores, which are stored locally in your browser (see Section 3).

2. How We Use Information

Because we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data to use, process, or share. Any anonymous, aggregated analytics (such as page view counts) may be used solely to understand overall site traffic and improve the user experience.

3. Local Storage & Cookies

Mini Games Hub uses your browser's localStorage to save mini game high scores and settings (e.g., sound on/off). This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is never transmitted to our servers
  • Can be deleted at any time by clearing your browser's site data

We may use minimal functional cookies required for advertising (see Section 5). These are set by Google, not by us directly.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect anonymous data:

  • Google Fonts — Loads web fonts. Google may log the request IP. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare (CDN / Font Awesome) — Delivers Font Awesome icons. See Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdSense — May display ads and set cookies (see Section 5).

We do not share personal data with any third party because we do not collect it in the first place.

5. Advertising (Google AdSense)

Mini Games Hub may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

For more information on how Google uses data from partner sites, see: How Google uses information from sites that use our services.

6. Children's Privacy

Mini Games Hub does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. Our games are family-friendly, but parents should supervise internet usage. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

7. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, most data-subject rights (access, erasure, portability) apply to your own browser's localStorage only. You can:

  • Clear all saved scores and settings by clearing your browser's storage for minigameshub.online
  • Opt out of Google ad personalisation via Google Ad Settings
  • Contact us at yadavdeepender68@gmail.com for any privacy questions

8. Security

The Mini Games Hub website is served over HTTPS. Because we do not collect or store personal data on servers, the risk of a data breach affecting your personal information is minimal. We regularly review the libraries and dependencies used on the site for security vulnerabilities.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any significant changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Mini Games Hub after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
